Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Introduction

Padelix ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Team Names and Match Settings: Stored on your device. Team names are not sent to Firebase Analytics or our servers.
  • User Preferences: App settings are stored on your device.

1.2 Usage Analytics

Firebase Analytics collects usage events while you use the App, including match actions and voice detection outcomes, as well as device and app information collected by the SDK. Analytics is enabled whenever the App starts, including after updating from a version in which you turned it off; there is no in-app analytics switch. Events use team labels (team 1, team 2, or none) and a voice-detected flag, not your team names or recognized speech text. Firebase may process identifiers and technical information under its own privacy policy; we do not describe this data as anonymous.

1.3 Voice Data

Voice scoring uses Apple's Speech recognition and requires microphone and speech recognition permissions. Speech recognition may use Apple's servers or a network connection, depending on Apple's processing and your device. We do not send audio recordings or recognized speech text to Firebase Analytics or our own servers. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles speech data.

1.4 Subscription and Payment Information

  • Apple processes App Store purchases and payments; we do not receive your payment card details.
  • Apphud handles subscription purchase and entitlement information so we can determine Pro access and restore purchases.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use local match settings for score tracking, purchase information for Pro access, and Firebase usage data to understand how the App is used and improve it. If you contact us, we use your message to respond to your request.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): Processes usage and technical data when you use the App. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apphud: Handles subscription purchases and entitlement status. Apphud Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store and Speech: Processes payments and may process speech recognition data over a network. Apple Privacy Policy

4. Storage and Security

Match settings and team names stay on your device and are not synchronized to our servers. You can remove local app data by deleting the App. Apple, Apphud, and Firebase handle their respective data under their own policies. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Subscription information is handled by Apple and Apphud; analytics data is processed by Firebase; speech data may be processed by Apple. We may disclose information when legally required.

6. Your Choices and Requests

The App does not provide an analytics switch. To stop future collection, you can stop using or delete the App; deleting it also removes locally stored match settings and team names. For questions or requests about data we control, including access or deletion, email us at galkin1928@gmail.com. You may also need to contact Apple, Apphud, or Google about data they process under their own policies.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below.

8. Data Retention

Local match settings remain on your device until you delete the App. Apple and Apphud retain purchase information according to their policies and legal obligations. Firebase retains collected data according to its settings and policy. Contact us about deletion of information we control; deleting the App does not remove previously collected data from third-party services.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy by publishing a new version here and changing the "Last Updated" date above.
