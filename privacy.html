Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 11, 2026

Introduction

PadelAudioCounter ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Team Names and Match Settings: Stored locally on your device, not transmitted to our servers
  • User Preferences: App settings, language preferences, subscription status

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, unique identifiers, app version
  • Usage Data: Features used, match statistics, app performance, crash reports
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous usage statistics via Firebase Analytics

1.3 Voice Data - Important Information

Your voice data is processed entirely on your device.

  • ✅ Voice recognition uses Apple's on-device Speech Recognition framework
  • ✅ We do NOT record, store, or transmit your voice data to our servers
  • ✅ Voice commands are processed locally for score tracking only
  • ⚠️ Apple may process voice data according to their privacy policy

1.4 Subscription and Payment Information

  • Processed by Apple through the App Store
  • We use Apphud SDK to manage subscription status
  • We do NOT collect or store your payment card information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality
  • Manage your subscription status
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Identify and fix bugs and technical issues
  • Develop new features based on user behavior
  • Send important updates and respond to support requests

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Firebase Analytics (Google)

  • Collects anonymous usage statistics
  • Privacy Policy

Apphud

  • Manages subscription status and in-app purchases
  • Privacy Policy

Apple App Store and Speech Recognition

  • Processes payments and subscriptions
  • May process voice data for speech recognition
  • Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • Match data, team names, and preferences are stored locally on your device
  • This data is NOT synchronized to cloud servers
  • You can delete this data by uninstalling the App

Security Measures

  • Encryption of data in transit
  • Secure storage practices
  • Regular security assessments
  • Limited access to personal information

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data.

We may share information only with:

  • Service providers (Firebase, Apphud) who assist in operating the App
  • Legal authorities when required by law
  • Business partners in case of merger or acquisition (with notice)

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of analytics tracking
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

GDPR Rights (European Users)

  • Right to data portability
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with supervisory authority

CCPA Rights (California Users)

  • Right to know what information is collected
  • Right to opt-out of sale (we don't sell your data)
  • Right to non-discrimination

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us immediately.

8. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Stored on your device until you delete the App
  • Analytics Data: Retained for 14-26 months (Firebase policy)
  • Subscription Data: Retained as required for billing and legal compliance

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you by:

  • Posting the new policy in the App
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending a notification for material changes
