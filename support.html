Frequently Asked Questions

How do I control the score with my voice?

Simply say the team name to add a point. Make sure your microphone is enabled and you're within hearing range of your device. You can customize voice commands in the app settings.

How do I undo a point?

Say "Undo" or tap the undo button on the scoreboard to reverse the last action.

The app doesn't recognize my voice commands

Make sure the microphone permission is granted in Settings > Padelix. Try speaking clearly and reducing background noise. You can also customize commands to words that are easier to recognize.

How do I restore my subscription?

Go to the app settings and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone > tap your name > Subscriptions > Padelix > Cancel Subscription. You'll retain access until the end of the current billing period.
